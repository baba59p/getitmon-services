bids, requests for quotes, confirmed customers, or exclusive leads**. The city
records project activity; it does not say a particular vendor remains unhired.

## Method and limits

Source: City of Austin [Issued Construction Permits](https://data.austintexas.gov/d/3syk-w9eu).
An initial query retrieved at most 50 commercial building permits issued from
September 21 through October 5, 2026. These three were then queried individually.
This is a capped sample, not complete city coverage. Status and dates are source
values. An issue date is not proof of project start; older applications can be
issued or reissued later. No completed date was supplied for these three records.
An absent completed date does not prove the work is unfinished.

No personal phone numbers, personal emails, or owner profiles are included.
City data are informational and may differ from official departmental records.
Check the official project record and actual buying stage before contacting a
business. The research is independent of, and not endorsed by, the City of Austin.
